## 👩‍💼 Executive Profile

> *"Empowering learners with the technologies of tomorrow — today."*

I am **Anita Budhiraja**, **Scientist-E**, **Associate Dean – Academics**, and **Head of Department (Training)** at **NIELIT Chandigarh/Ropar**, Main Campus of **NIELIT (Deemed to be University)** under the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India**. 

With over **three decades (30+ years)** of distinguished teaching, curriculum governance, software architecting, and scientific research leadership, I dedicate my work to bridging academic scholarship and national industrial readiness across **Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Big Data Analytics, Blockchain, IoT Security, and Immersive Technologies (AR/VR)**.

<br/>

**FutureSkills PRIME** *(Programme for Re-skilling / Up-skilling of IT Manpower for Employability)* is the premier **Central Sector Scheme** sanctioned by the **Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology (MeitY), Government of India**, implemented jointly by **NASSCOM, NIELIT, and C-DAC** across PAN India. The mission creates an institutional skilling ecosystem across ten identified emerging technology segments.

As **Chief Investigator**, I direct and execute two strategic domains:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                                 FUTURESKILLS PRIME NATIONAL DOMAINS                                    │

│ 📊 Big Data & Data Science       │ • Scalable Data Lakes & Distributed Compute (Hadoop / Spark)        │
│    (BDDS Lead)                   │ • End-to-End Predictive Analytics & Machine Learning Pipelines      │
│                                  │ • Python for High-Throughput Statistical Modeling & AI Research     │
├──────────────────────────────────┼─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 🥽 Augmented & Virtual Reality   │ • Spatial Computing & Immersive Application Engineering             │
│    (ARVR Lead)                   │ • 3D Environments, Interactive Simulations & Vision Integration     │
│                                  │ • Virtual Prototyping for Industry 4.0 & Healthcare Solutions       │
└──────────────────────────────────┴─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```
